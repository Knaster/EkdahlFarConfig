extractZeroCoefficientOffset (   equation)

Parses an equation to extract zeroPosition, coefficient, and offset, ensuring correct differentiation between components and retaining their signs.

Args: equation (str): The equation as a string.

Returns: dict: A dictionary with keys 'zeroPosition', 'coefficient', and 'offset'. Missing zeroPosition and offset default to 0, coefficient defaults to 1.
